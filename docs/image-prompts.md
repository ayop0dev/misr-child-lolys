### General Generation Guidelines
- **Bottle Specification**: A sleek 10 ml cylindrical travel-size clear perfume oil bottle, minimalist gold-metallic or satin-black cap, transparent oil showing pure golden amber or crystal-clear liquid with authentic fragrance ingredient illustration on the bottle.
- **Strict Quality**: High-end commercial beauty product photography, 8k resolution, photorealistic Hasselblad H6D-100c medium format camera look, sharp optical focus, soft creamy bokeh, professional studio and lifestyle lighting.
- **Rule**: Absolutely no human faces, no other brands' bottles, no text, no letters, no words, no watermarks, and no labels rendered inside the generated image.
